ABOUT ME

Turning complex workflows into clearer questions.

I am pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Healthcare Administration at the University of Phoenix, with a professional focus on patient access, revenue cycle support, healthcare analytics, and healthcare informatics.

My student portfolio explores how a small control gap at registration, eligibility verification, authorization, or documentation can lead to downstream rework. I use structured, evidence-based analysis to examine possible breakdown points, evaluate alternative explanations, and propose improvements that could be tested.

The emphasis is on traceability, accountability, and operational learning — not claims about real patients or professional outcomes.
