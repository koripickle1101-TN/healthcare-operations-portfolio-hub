Why This Matters to Me

From the patient side, eligibility, authorization, documentation, billing, and scheduling do not feel like separate departments. A problem in one part of the workflow can show up later as confusion or delay somewhere else. That connection is what I want to understand better.

01

How I Think About the Workflow

I follow the problem upstream. Instead of starting with the denial, delay, or rework at the end, I ask what happened earlier and where the first control was lost.

Patient Access Bottleneck Intelligence System™

Excel-based workflow map, case-control tracker, accuracy controls, and dashboard

I built this project to practice tracing where a front-end patient-access workflow first loses control. It connects a simulated registration inaccuracy with eligibility mismatch, authorization readiness, financial clearance, follow-up, escalation, patient communication, and verified closure.

I am practicing: Workflow mapping, data-quality review, prioritization, ownership, follow-up, escalation logic, documentation, closure controls, and interpreting simulated operational metrics.
