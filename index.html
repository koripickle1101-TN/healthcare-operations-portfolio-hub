Research and methodology

These references inform the educational design; they do not validate the simulations or certify compliance.

  • AHRQ Workflow Assessment for Health IT Toolkit — process mapping, handoffs and administrative workflow evaluation.
  • CMS-0057-F — applicable payer requirements and electronic authorization context, not proof of implementation. For non-drug items and services, specified impacted payers (excluding federally facilitated exchange QHP issuers) generally have maximum decision windows of 72 hours for expedited and seven calendar days for standard requests beginning in 2026; program-specific exceptions apply. API requirements generally begin in 2027. See CMS timeframe guidance.
  • Hamilton et al. (2023): healthcare ePortfolio scoping review and Janssens et al. (2022): eight-discipline ePortfolio scoping review — support documenting learning and competencies; neither establishes hiring outcomes for this portfolio.
  • WCAG 2.2 — accessibility testing target; conformance not established.

What this portfolio can—and cannot—demonstrate

The work illustrates how I structure hypothetical administrative problems, document missing information, identify review ownership, and examine synthetic data. It does not establish professional payer, EHR, coding, clinical, or healthcare employment experience. Modeled outcomes and scores are not proof of real-world operational improvement.

Some projects are still under audit. Earlier project documentation reports browser smoke and accessibility checks for PARCS and DPIS; current release results have not been independently reproduced in this review. A published repository does not mean the project has passed independent functionality, accessibility, or hiring-claims review. The resume page offers a printable copy and a Save as PDF option.
