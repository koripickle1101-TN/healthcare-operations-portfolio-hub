What this portfolio can—and cannot—demonstrate

The work illustrates how I structure hypothetical administrative problems, document missing information, identify review ownership, and examine synthetic data. It does not establish professional payer, EHR, coding, clinical, or healthcare employment experience. Modeled outcomes and scores are not proof of real-world operational improvement.

Some projects are still under audit. PARCS and DPIS passed their configured browser smoke and accessibility checks on review branches; these checks are not exhaustive application certification. A published repository does not mean the project has passed independent functionality, accessibility, or hiring-claims review. The resume page offers a printable copy and a Save as PDF option.
