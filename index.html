I built this portfolio to study where a healthcare workflow first loses control before that problem becomes a delay, another phone call, a rescheduled appointment, staff rework, billing confusion, or a denial.

As I work toward my Bachelor's of Science degree in Healthcare Administration, I use simulated projects to practice healthcare operations thinking without presenting student work as professional employment.
