Healthcare Operations Portfolio Hub is my master portfolio page connecting three healthcare operations systems I built: EVIS 1.0, PARCS 2.0, and DPIS 1.0.

Together, these projects show a complete front-end to mid-cycle revenue cycle workflow story:

Eligibility Verification → Prior Authorization → Denial Prevention

The portfolio focuses on eligibility risk, patient access risk, prior authorization reliability, claim readiness, denial prevention, revenue integrity, KPI design, executive reporting, and remote-ready healthcare operations documentation.

All data shown is synthetic and created for portfolio demonstration purposes. No patient information, protected health information, or real payer case data is used.
