# LinkedIn Launch Post

I built a Healthcare Operations Portfolio Hub to connect three portfolio systems I created around front-end and mid-cycle revenue cycle workflow improvement:

EVIS 1.0 — Eligibility Verification Intelligence System  
PARCS 2.0 — Prior Authorization Reliability Command System  
DPIS 1.0 — Denial Prevention Intelligence System

Together, the systems follow a workflow chain I wanted to study more deeply:

Eligibility Verification → Prior Authorization → Denial Prevention

The purpose of this portfolio is to show how front-end workflow defects can move downstream into authorization delays, claim denials, patient billing confusion, staff rework, and revenue leakage.

These projects demonstrate eligibility verification logic, patient access risk detection, prior authorization reliability, denial prevention thinking, KPI design, executive reporting, synthetic data design, and remote-ready healthcare operations documentation.

All data is synthetic. No patient information, protected health information, or real payer case data is used.
